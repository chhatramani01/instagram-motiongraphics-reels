# Cinematic Luxe Title

**Style ID:** `cinematic-luxe`  
**When to use:** Premium product unveil, branding, cinematic teasers.

## Visual fingerprint and asset direction
Near-black matte stage with metallic bronze accents, subtle depth haze, accurate lighting and clean product silhouette. Use **thin elegant serif + sturdy sans** as the typographic pairing (use available, properly licensed fonts with reliable fallbacks). Never recreate a particular influencer's branded asset, and never treat source screenshots as public-domain images. Design original shapes and illustrative metaphors for the topic.

## Numerical art direction
- Background `#131519`; primary foreground `#E9E8E2`; accent `#B69A66`; secondary `#58666F`. If content requires a warning/failure semantic, assign it explicitly rather than using a random color.
- Canvas defaults to `1080 × 1920` portrait, `30fps`. Hero text/object stays inside the conservative main safe zone x=9–86%, y=17–77%. Caption group should not bury the hero.
- Hierarchy: one dominant object, one source/proof element, one subordinate annotation. Use negative space to separate them.
- Composition: Single hero object occupies center, words no more than 2–5 per shot, proof via real details not arbitrary 3D rings.

## Motion grammar: implement as pure-time functions
Camera dolly slow 1.0→1.03, specular travel follows surface, main object settles 0.6s. Every item needs specified start/finish values and absolute seconds. Use actual analytic easing and sealed (pre-seeded) decorative noise to keep frame capture deterministic.

## Scene transitions and continuity
Shadow wipe motivated by the physical hero object or light occlusion. The exiting element should logically become or point at the next shot's main read. Use 4–8 frame directional wipes when there is no meaningful carry-over, not identical global crossfades after every sentence.

## Sound direction
short restrained sub hit, elegant cloth swish, spacious room tone. Choose cues only at meaningful state changes, on measured speech/beat events when source audio exists. If no voice was supplied, never claim the demo includes spoken narration.

## 12-second example skeleton
| Time | Visual | Reading task | Motion & SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5s | Hero contrast using this material | Recognize the problem/promise | Immediate object change / impact |
| 1.5–4.0s | Specific before state and why it fails | Understand stakes | Single meaningful relation moves |
| 4.0–7.3s | Mechanism built one piece at a time | See cause and effect | Connectors/transform tied to spoken clauses |
| 7.3–10.8s | Tangible result, artifact, or verified evidence | Understand payoff | Change of scale, one short soft-settle |
| 10.8–12.0s | Complete conclusion with readable hold | Remember outcome | Settled hero; quiet audio tail |

## Rejection checklist
- Reject a static text template with arbitrary zooms in place of a material-specific scene.
- Reject facts, logos or assets that were not provided or verified.
- Reject text clipped at 360px-wide mobile view or behind an Instagram side rail.
- Reject motion that resets context without an explanatory transition.
- Reject sound effects that compete with actual voiceover.
- Re-render contact sheet and final audio/video stream check after revisions.
