# Storyboard, hooks and retention system

**Evidence caution:** The techniques below are creative heuristics inspired by creator practice, not verified universal Instagram ranking rules. Do not promise viral reach or manipulate viewers with falsehoods.

## Hook family selection
| Type | Promise | Strong first image | First sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorial | 'You can use this' | Finished result / broken workflow contrast | Dry tick / marker hit |
| Personal story | 'I faced this' | Expression or consequence | Natural breath, understated impact |
| Myth-bust | 'You expected X, but Y' | Two visually contradictory objects | Crisp split-card snap |
| Evidence-first | 'Here is what actually happened' | Authentic receipt with source visible | Quiet page turn / spotlight cue |

## Exact timeline rules
First frame must be distinct. First second contains an intelligible visual idea rather than a cold logo. Between 1s and 4s, establish a concrete tension. Each 1–3s meaningful beat provides a new fact, mechanism, visual transformation, or direct proof. Leave a 0.8–1.2s hold for the final full takeaway. Loop only when naturally justified.

**Example (12s, informational, voice optional):**
| 0.0–1.2 | Hero statement + unexpected object motion | Promise | Short low thump |
| 1.2–3.3 | Compare before vs after | Contrast | Paper snap |
| 3.3–5.8 | Show first mechanical reason | Mechanism | Connector draw |
| 5.8–8.5 | Show the payoff in tangible artifact | Proof | Subtle click |
| 8.5–10.8 | Condense into one mental model | Remember | Soft rise |
| 10.8–12.0 | Hold final conclusion | Resolve | Tail/reverb |

## Ethical participation prompts
Ask useful questions or offer a genuine checklist. Deliberately wrong claims, false screenshots, impossible metrics and fake debate bait undermine trust. A short proof flash can invite a pause only if the full proof remains accessible and can be read elsewhere.

## Tone/format matrix
Educational → clear numbered system; personal → emotion and honest cost; product → verified before/after; news → source-first evidence; lyric → words as the choreography (only rights-cleared audio); talking-head → face first, visuals clarify rather than cover it.

## Final retention audit
- Is the central promise paid off?
- Is the hook relevant to the target viewer?
- Can a sound-off viewer identify the subject?
- Does every transition explain cause/contrast/continuity?
- Is there a screenshot-worthy takeaway?
