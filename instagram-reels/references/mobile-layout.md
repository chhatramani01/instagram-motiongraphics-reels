# 9:16 mobile composition and Reel-safe bands

Instagram app overlays change. These conservative design bands are **not official pixel guarantees** and must be checked on the actual destination account/app before publication.

**Reference canvas:** 1080 × 1920. Critical visual hero and captions should generally stay within x=108–929 and y=327–1478, reserving substantial top/bottom/side rail space. For upper-middle hook type, prefer y≈370–700. For grouped captions, often y≈1270–1450 with strong contrast. Avoid exact lower corners and right-side button region. Use proportionate coordinates so drafts at 360×640 remain legible.

- Minimum text legibility: verify mobile preview at 360px wide; simple hero words may need type 75–140px at 1080 design resolution. UI labels/annotations must remain recognizable at 1/3 scale.
- Safe composition requires negative space, not only a box around text. A moving figure and text must not collide at the transition midpoint.
- Avoid putting the speaker's face behind proof cards; use PIP or re-layout.
- Captions must not cover the focal artifact, particularly in demos/screenshots.
- Reassess 9:16 when repurposing for 4:5 or 1:1: reflow elements. Do not just resize a landscape design.
- Keep the Reel cover's central subject and headline safe for cropped grid thumbnails; inspect an actual grid crop separately.
