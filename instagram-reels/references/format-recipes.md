# Format recipes — choose at most one per Reel

These are creative workflows, not standalone code engines. In every case follow the main SKILL.md first, then the selected visual style.

## Faceless educational explainer
Start with a visual paradox; build a concrete diagram in 3–5 beats; keep one legible keyword per reveal. Use a whiteboard, editorial, blueprint or data-story style. Resolve with a checklist/mental model.

## Talking-head + proof cards
Keep a real supplied/authorized talking-head clip primary. Caption the voice, preserve faces, use windowed proof cards that enter only on relevant keywords. Avoid face-obscuring overlays and arbitrary B-roll.

## AI / product feature demo
Use actual source screenshots/recordings; show input action → visible response → verified benefit. Source text in screenshots may be too small; zoom/highlight specific regions while keeping source context. Never fake a product interface or performance number.

## News or evidence montage
Show a traceable source title/date before zooming to an exact phrase; pair narration claims only with relevant sourced proof. Avoid confusing an illustrative mockup for a genuine news screenshot.

## Personal founder story
Open on real stakes or a personal artifact; alternate honest friction and learning. Use a recurring object/character rather than anonymous stock. Pay off the opening failure with a concrete lesson.

## Kinetic typography
Use captions as actor: word building, grayscale-to-ink state, clean phrase transitions, proof object anchored to keyword. Keep typography under control; don't replace an actual explanation with a word salad.

## Music lyric video
Requires rights-cleared track and exact user-supplied lyrics/timestamps. Choreograph text to sung phrases, avoid truncating Devanagari matras, and don't use the audio as an unlicensed sample included in repo.

## Micro product launch
Reveal the user-verified name/function with deliberate cinematic material transformations. Contrast problem and concrete solution. Prefer original illustrations and authentic product assets to generic 3D-glow sphere reveals.
